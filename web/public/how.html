Get your Twitter / X archive

X lets you download your full history. It takes a day or two to arrive — here's the whole flow.

1

Request your archive

On X, go to Settings → Your account → Download an archive of your data. You'll confirm your password and a verification code. X usually has it ready within ~24 hours, sometimes a few days.

Open X download page ↗
2

Download the .zip

X notifies you (and emails you) when it's ready. Open the notification and download the .zip to your computer. It can be large if you have a lot of media — that's fine, moots only reads the text.

3

Drop it into moots

Come back here and drop the whole .zip — or just the tweets.js file inside data/ — onto the page. Your constellation builds instantly.

Open moots ↗
🔒 Your archive never leaves your device. moots parses everything locally in your browser — no tweets are uploaded. Only public handles are sent to fetch profile pictures.

✦ While you're at it — feed the Community Archive

The Community Archive is an open, shared dataset of Twitter history that powers research and fun tools like this one. If you're comfortable making your public tweets openly available, consider uploading your archive there too — it helps the whole ecosystem.

Already in it? You can download your archive.json from the Community Archive and drop it straight into moots — it reads that format directly.
